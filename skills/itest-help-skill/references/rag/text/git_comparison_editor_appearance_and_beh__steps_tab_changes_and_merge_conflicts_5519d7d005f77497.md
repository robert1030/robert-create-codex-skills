# Comparison editor appearance and behavior > Comparison editor appearance and behavior > Comparing two test cases > Steps tab: Changes and Merge Conflicts

The screen-shots below shows examples of merge conflicts on the Testcase Editor > Steps tab and how you may resolve merge conflicts, manually changes properties one at a time or change all.

The merge conflict indicator appears on the right of the test case steps on both the compare editors. You may expand the collapsed test case steps and navigate to the step property causing the merge conflict as shown in the screenshot below.

You may resolve conflict manually by selecting each conflicting property or by clicking arrow button on the center panel.

In the example below, the merge conflict indicator appears on the right of the procedure. You may expand the collapsed procedure steps to view the nested steps causing the merge conflict as shown in the screenshot below.

Resolve Merge Conflicts: Select the property that has a merge conflict indicator, an arrow appears in the middle of the two comparison windows. Click the arrow to accept and merge the conflict or manually update the property.

Changes: Click the arrow on the top right of the source window to copy all non-conflicting properties or change the properties manually.
