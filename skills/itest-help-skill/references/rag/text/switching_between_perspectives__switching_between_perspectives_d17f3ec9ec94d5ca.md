# Switching between perspectives > Switching between perspectives

To switch to a different perspective, click Switch Perspective and then select it from the list. Once you use a perspective, its button remains at the top (right-hand side of the iTest window ) so you can click to switch perspectives at any time. You may also navigate to different perspectives as shown below.
